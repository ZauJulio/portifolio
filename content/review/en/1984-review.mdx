# 1984

Orwell's genius isn't the boot stamping on a human face — it's the paperwork, the language, the
quiet erosion of what a person is allowed to think. _Newspeak_, _doublethink_, _Big Brother_: the
book didn't just describe a nightmare, it handed us the words to recognize it.

## What worked

- Winston's diary — the smallest possible act of rebellion — makes the stakes intimate and human.
- The Ministry of Truth's rewriting of history feels more relevant now, not less.
- The appendix on Newspeak turns linguistics itself into horror.

## What didn't

The middle section's political tract slows the pulse, and the romance is more device than warmth.
But the ending's cold, total defeat is the point — and it lands like nothing else in the genre.
